    About

    I'm a Brazilian software engineer and I've been building software since 2007. My background is mostly frontend and mobile, but I like working across the whole stack, taking on hard problems and turning ideas into real products.

    Lately, AI has become a big part of what I do. At Civic we adopted AI early in our development workflow, and later I got to build AI products themselves: a secure MCP platform for agents and an AI go-to-market tool. I'm excited about what it makes possible and skeptical of it as a magic solution. What interests me is using it to solve real problems. On the side I tinker with agent orchestration and LLM tooling.

    Outside work I care about social and environmental issues: reducing poverty, improving access to essential services and opportunities, and protecting the environment. I'm also fond of open source and of projects that do some good.

    I'm based in Brazil (UTC-3), I prefer fully remote roles, and I'm available now for part-time and freelance work.
